# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

AxonOS is built in the open by people who care about doing hard engineering
honestly. We want participation in the project to be a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being respectful, patient, and welcoming — especially to newcomers;
- giving and accepting constructive, technical feedback in good faith;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments;
- personal or political attacks;
- publishing others' private information without permission;
- sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Honesty is part of conduct

Because trust is the project's foundation, knowingly misrepresenting results —
presenting unmeasured claims as measured, or passing off others' work as your
own — is treated as a conduct issue, not just a technical one.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, and
participants that violate this code. To report a concern, email
**connect@axonos.org**. Reports will be handled confidentially and in good faith.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
